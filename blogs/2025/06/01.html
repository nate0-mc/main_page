My Experience with Anime

            posted: June 8, 2025

Being a teenager, anime has been one of those things that most of us have at least watched at some time. No matter what kind of anime it is, we all watched at least one show.

However... my experience with anime was a bit more weird. Back then, before I was even a teenager, I was certain I would never like anime. Later on, I figured it was actually a good idea to watch ONE anime show because it had some form of representation to myself. The rest was history. Kind of a bleak summary, but I promise, that's how it all went if I could summarize it all.

If the way I am writing this article sounds dramatic, that's both on purpose and by accident.

⇒ Before 2024

Before, I was too rebellious against anime in general. As a child, I found it something that girls would only like (well look who's talking 6 years later.) Or, something that seemed too cringe. I only viewed anime as Naruto, D.B.Z, or My Hero Academia. I figured they were kinda overrated, but as a child, I thought that feeling of overratedness aligned with "corny" or "cringe".

Throughout my primary school, I physically gagged looking at any image that included anime. I would look away, refusing to even try to listen to a second of background noise from a random anime show. Why? Childish behavior. I never really found it surprising that I acted like this, because children have strong opinions against things e.g the existence of Santa, or any sort of brainrot that children watch nowadays (Italian brainrot as of writing.)

This sort of strengthened on in the pandemic. Everywhere I went, YouTube, or any post I find, there was a sprinkle of anime hidden. I had a physical reaction that I can't seem to recreate. Especially since almost all day during 2020-21 was spent inside, I had to be cautious.

My friends liked to tease me by sending GIFs of anime in any group chat we were in, and I found it disgusting (for whatever reason it is.) Nowadays I know why. It was childish, repeating what I said earlier. Well... in 2023, I sort of, let the hatred loose. Sure I still did not like the idea of watching or let alone looking at a PNG of an anime girl, but I sort of tried to endure it. Of course I had the occasional tease that I back off sometimes, but it's there. I just had no idea how to describe it at all.

⇒ When it all shattered

2024. New me. Obviously. I found out I was trans in this year, and, for some reason, I found it "feminine" to watch anime. I kinda have no idea why, maybe because a lot of posts or r/egg_irl all kind of have an anime style? Maybe, I am unsure. I had my desires straight, even if I was reluctant. The moment my hatred for anime shattered, was actually during a field trip on the way back home.

I will let that slip. The first anime that I have ever watched was Fate Apocrypha. Mostly because of Astolfo. He was just cute. And I found him a cool character in general (mostly because before I was trans, I was a femboy... and I found him kinda hot.) The anime was good for me, I don't want to get as to why.

My friends could not believe the fact that I was watching anime. In the gaming industry we called that character development. They were recommending me all sorts of anime, but when I was starting out, I really just wanted an anime that had some sort of LGBTQ+ main character, more specifically one who is trans/non-binary.

The second anime I ever watched was That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime. My first isekai1 anime too. The main character was non-binary, not trans, but I still loved it. I devoured the entire thing within two days, and it felt amazing.

At that point I was deep within the depths of what I hated before as a child. However... I was out of options for what to watch next.

I usually Wikipedia some random stuff I know when I am bored, and I found out that Fate Apocrypha is actually a part of the Fate franchise. Considerably, I was interested.

I just watched Fate: Stay Night, by Studio Deen since it was the first anime adaptation of the visual novel. Do not watch it. It sucks. I stopped like five episodes in. I instead watched Unlimited Blade Works. I was confused because at first they had the same story (I was wrong). But I figured anyway, because it was more recent as it looked like it had good animation.

10/10. I loved watching it. I watched Heaven's Feel next, and oh my god, the way it's presented it makes it 12/10. I loved every character. I really found myself in a rabbit hole I can't escape.

I then watched a lot of Fate media; Today's Menu for the Emiya Family, Kaleid Prisma Illya, Grand Order, Last Encore, and parts of Zero. I was deep.

⇒ My First Favorite

I was kinda getting bored of watching Fate, and I was just watching random videos, until I found this video by GiggUk. I searched his channel for curiosity, and I found a video that talks about a certain anime that I found kinda weird in the idea. A guy, who has, 100 girlfriends. Sounds tropey, but that's the point.

The 100 Girlfriends Who Really, Really, Really, Really, Really Love You was my fourth anime. Just call it Hyakkano, it's shortened of the romanized name. I actually found the anime, EXTREMELY funny. To the point where it brought me out of my depression for 3 hours. It was written as a joke harem anime, where the main character doesn't have to choose or isn't biased to one or two girlfriends. Just watch it. It is insanely funny.

Back then, I was deep within that anime. I rewatched the show numerous times. I ended up reading the manga, and sort of sped through it for a day. It had like 201 or something chapters when I was done. I was extremely satisfied. The girlfriends who weren't even in the anime (it only had 1 season then) were also pretty hilarious.

⇒ 2025

Kinda still deep in the Hyakkano fandom, but I just watched more anime day by day. I've became a partial weeb, because of reasons I do not want to delve into (I promise it's not porn!) Anyway, a couple months have passed, and I have watched a lot of anime by then:

  • Zombieland Saga
  • Onimai
  • Senpai is an Otokonoko
  • Frieren: Beyond Journey's End
  • ISEKAI QUARTET
  • Konosuba

I was kinda getting bored. Anime just became another genre that just patches my soul with a temporary hit of dopamine.

A couple months have passed, and I just finished Konosuba. I found about a certain anime that I feel like almost anyone knows. Considering my intense desire to just watch something that can let me escape such a hard time in my life back then, I wanted to watch something funny. It's Bocchi the Rock!, I don't need tension.

⇒ My First Comfort Anime

My friend actually showed me this anime, and he was an addict to this show in particular too. I watched the first two episodes and kinda got distracted watching Konosuba. However, I went back, and I watched the entire season.

Best thing I have ever watched. The humor, characters (and their contrasts), music, animation, and relatability (social anxiety and introversion) play into the anime's high reception.

The same anime that introduced my new favorite character (comfort character, first character to help me cope with my MDD, and current waifu hehe) Ryo Yamada. The anime lived true to it's claims that she can make women fall for her. She made me bisexual.2

Now this title might be a little misleading. I have not rewatched the anime, for, uhh,, "other reasons" but I would often scroll through the subreddit r/BocchiTheRock and/or through, coughs Twitter. Dogshit app by the way. Anyways, this was the first anime that made me cope with my pretty much endless spiral of depression. Not edgy at all, genuinely do have depression. I have not seen an anime that I have watched since Hyakkano to bring me out of the stages of pain until this one. Hyakkano was almost my comfort anime, it just had some imperfections. Nowadays it's just overused coping; it doesn't affect me, but I still consider it to be my first coping and comfort anime.

The impacts of this anime kinda show. I have copied some behavioral patterns of my now all-time favorite character, introduced me to rock music (kinda weird I know), and made me get into playing instruments altogether.

Another longterm effect was the fact: this anime also started a band within my class. We have four people (or five) in my class whom I am close friends with, and we all watched this anime. We all loved it. One of them decided to start a band, and I am in it too (the other four are in there; I am the vocalist haha.)

⇒ The Present

Right now, still deep in MDD (Major Depressive Disorder) I have been deep in this anime rabbit hole. If you're derogatory enough, you can call me a weeb. I have watched more anime: My Deer Friend Nokotan, Horimiya, Girls Band Cry, So I'm a Spider So What?, I'm In Love With the Villainess, Kaguya-Sama: Love Is War (Season 1) and currently watching Komi Can't Communicate.

Currently, I am trying to find an anime that represents suicidal and depressive nature (because I need that relatability complex). Currently, anime is one of the three things that keep me alive by a thread (other two is playing music, and waifu; as corny and cringe as it is.) If there is any anime that you may recommend (cannot be "generally popular" anime like Jujutsu Kaisen or Attack on Titan, or SPYxFAMILY. I have my reasons.) then you can just ping me in my Discord server, and tell me the anime. I will put it on a list.

⇒ Remarks

I believe my greatest mistake as a child was to not watch anime. I feel like I would've been happier, or less depressed in the present. Anime has been a coping mechanism for a while now, and honestly I am starting to addicted to it. I don't mind; addiction to a way to cope kinda makes it a non-coping mechanism anymore because you're tired of it. Anyway, another remark I can make is that: I need to watch more anime. I don't really like isekai stuff that much nowadays, and I love the kinds of anime that make me smile once in a while, or the anime that brings my emotions out.

So sorry for the intense gap between my first blog. I had no motivation because, again, mental health and other stuff. I hope more I have the motivation to write more blogs once school starts, because more stories.
