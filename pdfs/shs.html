Information

This houses all of the PDFs from Year 5 and 6. Unlike Junior High, my current school has a trimester system. There are two summative assessments, and one end term exam every trimester. A summative assessment is just a smaller exam. An end term exam is an exam about the overall trimester.

The first term has two math classes: General Mathematics and Finite Mathematics. Finite Mathematics is an elective. General Mathematics is a year-long subject. I try to update the reviewers every assessment or exam period, so that means the documents will be fully packed of topics.

The lectures move topics much faster than my previous school, so there will be a lot more in each reviewer. Expect close to a hundred pages.

These guides are free. Do not repurpose for commercial use. Reprinting for personal use is allowed.
